I built this AI lead qualification and follow-up system for service businesses. It automatically qualifies, scores, and follows up with leads while updating the CRM as soon as a form is submitted. From form submission to booked call, the entire process runs with minimal manual intervention.

Service businesses no longer have to waste time manually qualifying leads or risk losing deals because of slow or inconsistent follow-up.

Here's what happens the moment someone submits a form:

- **Automatic CRM updates:** The system captures the lead's information and updates the CRM without manual data entry.
- **AI qualification:** AI analyzes the submission and determines whether the lead is a good fit.
- **Lead scoring and prioritization:** A scoring model ranks leads and identifies high-priority prospects.
- **Personalized follow-up:** Leads receive an automated follow-up sequence tailored to their score and qualification results.
- **Objection handling:** The system answers questions and addresses concerns before guiding prospects toward booking a call.

No lead falls through the cracks. No one waits hours for an initial response. And the sales team spends less time on repetitive administrative work and more time on the conversations that matter.

This isn't a chatbot bolted onto a website. It's an end-to-end sales workflow connecting lead capture, CRM updates, qualification, scoring, follow-up, and booking.

If leads are coming in faster than your team can respond, this system helps you keep up without adding more manual work.
