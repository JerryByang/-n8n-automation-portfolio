I built this AI lead qualification and follow-up system for service businesses. It qualifies, scores, and follows up with leads automatically. From form submission to booked call, with zero manual work in between.

Service businesses don't have to waste time qualifying leads or lose deals because of slow or inconsistent follow-up anymore.

Here's what happens the moment someone submits an inquiry:

-AI reads their submission and decides if they're a genuine fit.

-A scoring model then ranks the leads and filters high priority leads from low priority

-Leads get an instant, personalized follow-up cadence based on their lead score, until they convert

-The system also handles questions and objections leads have prior to booking

No lead falls through the cracks. No one waits hours for a reply. And the sales team only spends time on the conversations that actually matter.

This isn't a chatbot bolted onto a website. It's a full pipeline: qualification, scoring, routing, follow-up, and handoff, all working together.

If leads are coming in faster than your team can respond, this is the fix.
